# Dubai event car guide

Planning a car for a wedding, a corporate event or a photoshoot in Dubai? These notes cover the points that are easiest to forget.

## Before the day

1. Write down the schedule: pickup time, each stop, and the end time.
2. Confirm who the driver will be and how to reach them.
3. Ask what the booking includes, such as waiting time, extra hours and parking.
4. Check road and parking rules with the [Roads and Transport Authority](https://www.rta.ae/) and the [UAE government portal](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/traffic-and-road-safety).
